Day One

Admission Day — {{ config.admission_date }}

Step-by-step guide for your admission day. Read fully the night before.

Navigate How to Reach ISI

If coming by cab, paste the Google Maps link shared by seniors directly. If by public transport (bus/auto), ask for "203 Gate, ISI Kolkata" — it's opposite the ISI Bus Stop. Do NOT get off at 205 Gate/Bonhooghly (that's the hostel gate — you'll walk unnecessarily).

Timing Arrive by 9:30 AM

Try to reach early. Ask the security guard for "SN Bose Bhawan", take the lift to the 5th floor. Parents may accompany you initially — confirm waiting arrangements with officials.

Documents Inside the Admission Room

Officials will guide you to a specific room. Keep all documents ready — originals, photocopies, and photos. Listen carefully and stay alert about where to sign on any forms.

Hostel Ask for Hostel Allocation Form

If you're opting for hostel accommodation, ask the officials specifically for the hostel allocation form. Don't forget — they won't always prompt you.

Verify One-on-One Document Check

You'll have a short interaction with an official — show all documents clearly and properly. Be honest about where you're from when speaking to hostel wardens. Don't bend the truth without valid reason.

Weather Carry an Umbrella!

Kolkata gets heavy rain in July. The hostel gate area may be waterlogged. Carry an umbrella or raincoat without fail — especially for out-of-state students.

Backup Soft Copies on Phone

Keep soft copies of all documents on your phone. If you need a printout urgently, there are xerox shops near 205 Gate and near Baranagar Metro — seniors will drop locations in the group.

Stipend Setup

Aadhaar Seeding for Stipend

For your stipend to reach you, your Aadhaar must be seeded (not just linked) to your bank account and mapped with NPCI for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

What to Take to the Bank

  1. Two filled Aadhaar seeding forms (from dbtbharat.gov.in)
  2. Photocopy of your Aadhaar card
  3. Any other documents the bank may request
Key tip: Submit one form to the bank; get the second form stamped & signed by the bank official as proof of submission. Politely ask for the IIN (Issuer Identification Number) of your branch. Many officials won't know it — escalate to a senior official if needed.

Note: IIN is NOT the first 6 digits of your debit card. Use only the NPCI website to verify.
NPCI Live Banks List ↗ DBT Bharat Forms ↗

How to Verify Seeding Status

  1. Log in to the UIDAI website with your Aadhaar and check seeding status
  2. Check NPCI's Aadhaar Mapper Status page
On Admission Day: Carry the stamped + signed Aadhaar seeding form with you to document verification. If you already seeded Aadhaar earlier, carry printout screenshots of your UIDAI and NPCI verification pages as well.
If you want to be safe, you can wait for ISI to officially notify you about this process — the forms are standard and remain the same each year.
