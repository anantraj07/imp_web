Eat Well

Food & Mess Guide

Timings, locations, and how things work at ISI Kolkata's mess and canteens.

🌅

Breakfast — BHos Mess

8:30–11:00 AM
Head to the ground floor of the boys' hostel (CV Raman Hall)
Find the queue & register with name list
Sign under the "Breakfast" column beside your name
Take a plate and collect your food
Lunch served on weekends only from the same mess.
🌙

Dinner — BHos Mess

8:30–10:30 PM
Same location as breakfast — ground floor BHos
Sign under the "Dinner" column in the register
Collect meal at the counter
Evening & night canteen also operates from here — great for late-night snacks.
☀️

Lunch — 2nd Floor Canteen

12:55–2:15 PM
Located at Rani Bhavan (College Campus)
Go to the 2nd floor — expect long queues, go early
Check daily menu posted on the wall
Order directly — payment via cash or UPI
Full thali available: rice, sabzi, dal, etc.
🏛️

Lunch — 1st Floor (Govt Canteen)

12:55–2:15 PM
Also at Rani Bhavan — 1st floor
Ask for a coupon at the counter first
Cheaper with limited variety — no rice usually
Roti, noodles, pasta available
Best if on a tight budget. Minimal cafeteria setup.
Your Room

Hostel Room Guide

Floor and wing recommendations from seniors who know which rooms actually get sleep.

Floor-wise Rankings

2nd Floor BEST Avoids insects AND summer heat — the sweet spot
1st Floor GOOD Better than ground floor; some mosquito exposure
3rd Floor OK Hot in summer, but may beat ground floor this semester
Ground Floor AVOID Closest to mess ✓ but mosquitoes are brutal in monsoon
East ≫ North > West ≈ South
General wing preference — some rooms may be exceptions based on ventilation.

Before You Finalize a Room

  • Room hygiene and exact location
  • Furniture — bed, table, and chair condition
  • Washroom condition (shared) — contact washroom partner if possible
  • Almirah condition
  • Working lights, fans, exhaust fans
  • Bookshelf availability
  • Overall ventilation and natural light
Minor issues (lights, fans) can be fixed via a requisition — don't reject a room solely for them. Focus on livability.

Communicate your room number options to the group when you receive them — seniors can guide better with specifics.
